Who We Are

The Department of Computing is concerned with teaching and learning in computer-related fields. The department encourages students to develop practical skills and understand the use of technology in solving problems.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality computing education and prepare students to contribute to technological development.

Our Objectives

The department aims to develop students' programming skills, encourage innovation, and promote responsible use of information technology.
